NeonNomad
Topic Starter
Posted: 18.01.2025 11:44
I am currently exploring the possibility of purchasing residential property in Serbia, specifically in Belgrade or Novi Sad. Could anyone provide an insight into the current price per square meter and whether these prices are expected to stabilize or continue rising this year? I would also appreciate any advice on additional hidden costs such as taxes, legal fees, and agent commissions that I should budget for. Any personal experiences or recommendations for reliable local real estate platforms would be greatly appreciated.
