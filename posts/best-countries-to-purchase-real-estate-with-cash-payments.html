Best countries to purchase real estate with cash payments

WanderingWarlock
Topic Starter
Posted: 27.01.2025 11:07
I am currently looking to invest my savings into international real estate and prefer to avoid complex mortgage processes or financing hurdles. I am interested in knowing which countries have transparent legal frameworks that allow for legitimate cash purchases by foreign nationals. Are there any specific regions where paying in cash simplifies the transaction, or should I be wary of money laundering regulations? Any advice or personal experiences regarding smooth cash-based property acquisitions abroad would be greatly appreciated.
