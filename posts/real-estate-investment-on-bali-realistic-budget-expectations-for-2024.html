XenialXenon
Topic Starter
Posted: 19.01.2025 01:07
I am currently exploring the possibility of purchasing property in Bali for both personal use and potential rental income. I have heard conflicting information regarding entry-level prices for villas versus apartments in popular areas like Canggu or Uluwatu. Could someone provide a realistic estimate of what it costs to get a decent property today, including potential hidden fees or taxes? I would appreciate advice on whether it is better to buy off-plan or look for resale properties in the current market.
