Best countries to invest in real estate for residency (Golden Visa programs)?

NebulaNavigator
Topic Starter
Posted: 21.01.2025 09:04
I am currently looking to diversify my assets by investing in international real estate, ideally in a country that offers a clear path to residency or a Golden Visa program. I have a budget of around $250,000 and am looking for stable markets with good potential for rental yield or long-term appreciation. Could anyone share their recent experiences or recommendations on which countries currently offer the most straightforward and reliable paths to legal residency through property investment? Any advice on the current tax implications or hidden costs to watch out for would be greatly appreciated.
