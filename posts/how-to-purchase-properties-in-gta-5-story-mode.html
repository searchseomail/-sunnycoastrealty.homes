How to purchase properties in GTA 5 Story Mode?

IronIconoclast
Topic Starter
Posted: 13.01.2025 12:42
I have been playing GTA 5 for a while now and I see icons on the map for businesses that can be bought. However, I am not sure exactly how to initiate the purchase or if there are specific requirements for each character. Could someone explain the step-by-step process for buying real estate and if it is worth the investment for passive income? Any tips on which properties provide the best return would be greatly appreciated.

NomadicNinja
Replied: 31.01.2025 08:23
To buy property, just walk up to the 'For Sale' sign icon on the map. You'll see a prompt to press right on the D-pad to purchase it. It's pretty straightforward!

Replied: 28.02.2025 11:35
Passive income is great, but don't expect to get rich overnight. Some properties pay off better than others, kind of like looking for the best coastal locations to invest in property in real life—you have to do your research first.
SolarSurfer
Replied: 10.03.2025 02:16
Most properties are character-specific, but some can be bought by anyone. The golf club is the most expensive but generates a decent weekly payout if you play long-term.
EchoEnigma
Replied: 26.03.2025 03:18
Make sure you have enough cash on hand. If you haven't finished the main story yet, I'd wait until the final heist so you have the capital to invest in the big businesses.

Buying property is fun, but just remember it's not like buying Property in Belarus as a Russian citizen; there's no complicated legal paperwork here, just click and buy!
EmberExplorer

The best tip I can give is to wait until you have plenty of money from the big heists. Buying businesses early on is a waste of money because the return on investment takes forever.
NeonNomad
