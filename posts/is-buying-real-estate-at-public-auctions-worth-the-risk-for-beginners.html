Is buying real estate at public auctions worth the risk for beginners?

ZenithZephyr
Topic Starter
Posted: 02.01.2025 06:42
I am looking into purchasing a property through a government auction to save on costs, but I have heard about many legal pitfalls. Does anyone have experience with the process of buying real estate at auctions and how to properly verify the property's status beforehand? I am worried about hidden liens or squatters and would appreciate advice on how to mitigate these risks.
