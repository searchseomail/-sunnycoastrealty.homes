Topic Starter

I have been playing GTA 5 for a while now and I am looking to expand my empire by buying properties like businesses or garages. Could someone guide me on the specific steps to buy real estate in the single-player campaign? Are there any specific missions I need to complete before these options become available?

You need to progress through the story until you finish the mission 'Nervous Ron' with Trevor. After that, property markers will start appearing on your map as little house icons with a dollar sign.

Just walk up to the 'For Sale' sign outside the business or property. It’s way easier than trying to navigate the in-game websites, which are mostly for show in single-player.
