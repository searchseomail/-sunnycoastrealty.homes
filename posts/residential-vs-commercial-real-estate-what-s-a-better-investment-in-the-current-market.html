Residential vs Commercial Real Estate: What's a better investment in the current market?

XenialXenon
Topic Starter
Posted: 12.02.2025 16:09
I have managed to save up a decent amount of capital and I am trying to decide whether I should buy a residential apartment for long-term renting or invest in commercial real estate instead. Commercial properties seem to offer higher initial rental yields, but they also appear to have higher vacancy risks and management costs. On the other hand, residential apartments are much easier to liquidate if I suddenly need cash. What factors should I weigh most heavily before making a final decision?
