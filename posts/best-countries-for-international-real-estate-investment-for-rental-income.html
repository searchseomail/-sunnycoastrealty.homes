Best countries for international real estate investment for rental income?

ShadowScribe
Topic Starter
Posted: 12.02.2025 06:39
I am looking to diversify my portfolio by purchasing property abroad specifically for long-term rental income. I have a budget of around $200k and am prioritizing countries with stable legal frameworks and high tourism demand. Could anyone share their experience or recommendations on which markets currently offer the best ROI for buy-to-let properties? I am particularly interested in Europe or Southeast Asia, but I am open to other suggestions as well.
