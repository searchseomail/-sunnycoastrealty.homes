Investing Maternity Capital into Real Estate: What are the Current Options?

ZenithZephyr
Topic Starter
Posted: 28.01.2025 12:10
I am looking for advice on how to effectively use our maternity capital certificate for a property purchase. Are there specific restrictions on the types of residential properties we can buy, or can it be applied to any housing? I would also like to know if it is possible to use these funds for a down payment on a mortgage or if there are better ways to invest it. Any guidance or personal experiences from those who have already navigated this process would be greatly appreciated.

Yes, you can absolutely use maternity capital as a down payment for a mortgage. The main restriction is that the property must be located within the country and be classified as 'residential.' You cannot use it for commercial real estate or land plots without a house.

We used our certificate for a mortgage down payment last year. It was a straightforward process, but ensure the title is in the name of both parents or the child. If you are considering international options, you might find this guide on buying property abroad as a non-resident interesting for future planning.

Just a heads up: you cannot buy a house that is officially condemned or slated for demolition. The pension fund will verify the property status before approving the funds transfer, so always get an independent appraisal first.

My advice is to apply the funds directly to the principal of your mortgage once the loan is active. It saves a massive amount of interest over the long term compared to using it just for the initial deposit.

Remember that you are legally required to allocate a share of ownership to your children. This is a common pitfall that trips people up during the registration process, so consult with a notary early on.

It depends on your local laws, but generally, it must be a self-contained residential unit. You can't use it to buy a share of a room in a communal apartment usually, so double-check the 'residential' status of the specific unit.
