What is the minimum investment amount for Turkish residency and citizenship in 2024?

VortexVagabond
Topic Starter
Posted: 08.01.2025 03:52
I am currently exploring the Turkish real estate market and am confused about the current thresholds for obtaining a residence permit versus citizenship. Could someone clarify the exact investment amounts required for each program as of this year? I have heard conflicting information regarding whether the valuation needs to be in USD or Lira. Any advice or recent experiences from expats who have successfully navigated this process would be greatly appreciated.

PlasmaProwler
Replied: 18.01.2025 15:44
For Turkish citizenship by investment, the current threshold is $400,000 USD. For a residence permit, the real estate value must be at least $200,000 USD in metropolitan areas. It must be in USD, as the Land Registry office uses the Central Bank's exchange rate on the day of the deed transfer.

I went through the process last year. The $400k for citizenship is non-negotiable. For residency, it is $200k. You need an official appraisal report (SPK license) which converts the Lira value to USD at the official rate. Don't rely on unofficial sources.
