Current property prices in Turkey: Is it still affordable for foreigners?

SilverSentinel
Topic Starter
Posted: 01.01.2025 03:53
I have been considering investing in real estate in Turkey for a while now, but I am confused by the recent price fluctuations. Could someone provide a realistic overview of what a decent apartment costs in popular areas like Antalya or Alanya today? I would also appreciate information on the additional hidden costs, such as taxes and legal fees, that I should factor into my budget. Any advice from recent buyers would be greatly appreciated.

Don't forget the hidden costs! You need to budget an extra 10-12% on top of the purchase price for Title Deed fees (Tapu), notary costs, translation services, and property purchase tax.

Alanya is slightly more affordable than central Antalya, but the residency permit situation is becoming very strict. Make sure you check which neighborhoods are currently closed to foreigners before putting down a deposit.

I bought in Alanya last year. The property price was $130,000, but between the lawyer fees, the appraisal report (SPK), and the mandatory DASK earthquake insurance, I ended up paying nearly $15,000 in extra fees.

As a recent buyer, my advice is to focus on the 'Expertise Report'. The Turkish government requires an official valuation for foreigners, and the price you pay must align with this report to qualify for residency.

Is it still affordable? Honestly, for a middle-class foreigner, the cost of living combined with the property price threshold for residency makes it a challenging investment. Do your math on the ROI carefully.
