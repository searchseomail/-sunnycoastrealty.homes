Which countries currently allow real estate purchases using cryptocurrency?

ObsidianOracle
Topic Starter
Posted: 13.02.2025 16:13
I am looking to diversify my investment portfolio by purchasing property abroad using my crypto holdings. I have heard that some jurisdictions are becoming more crypto-friendly, but I am struggling to find reliable information on where this is legally possible and straightforward. Does anyone have experience with buying real estate directly with Bitcoin or stablecoins in countries like the UAE, Portugal, or others? I would appreciate advice on the legal hurdles and the best regions to look into.

The UAE, specifically Dubai, is currently the leader here. Many developers now accept crypto, but it usually goes through a licensed broker who converts it to fiat immediately. It's not a direct 'wallet-to-wallet' transfer in most cases.

Portugal is very crypto-friendly regarding taxes, but buying property directly with Bitcoin is legally complex. You typically have to convert to Euros via a local exchange first to satisfy the notary requirements for the deed.
