Which countries offer citizenship by real estate investment in 2024?

NebulaNavigator
Topic Starter
Posted: 08.01.2025 14:12
I am currently looking to diversify my assets and am interested in purchasing property abroad to secure a second passport. Could anyone recommend countries that currently have reliable 'Citizenship by Investment' programs linked to real estate? I am specifically looking for jurisdictions with a stable legal framework and a clear path to naturalization. Any personal experiences or advice on the tax implications of these investments would be greatly appreciated.
