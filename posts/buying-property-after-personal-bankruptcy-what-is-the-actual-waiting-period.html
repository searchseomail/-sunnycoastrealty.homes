Buying property after personal bankruptcy: What is the actual waiting period?

LunarLurker
Topic Starter
Posted: 28.01.2025 07:12
I recently completed my bankruptcy proceedings and received a discharge, but I am now looking to purchase a home in the near future. I am confused about the specific waiting periods required by mortgage lenders before they will consider a new application. Does the timeline differ significantly between government-backed loans and conventional financing? Any advice or personal experiences regarding how long I should wait to improve my chances of approval would be greatly appreciated.

The waiting periods depend heavily on the loan type. For FHA loans, it's generally two years from the discharge date for Chapter 7, while conventional loans through Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac typically require four years. I went through this five years ago. My advice is to focus on rebuilding your credit score immediately after discharge. Even if you meet the two-year FHA minimum, lenders will look at your credit history post-bankruptcy, so keep those balances low and pay everything on time.

Conventional loans are much stricter than government-backed ones. If you try for a conventional loan, expect to wait at least four years. If you need a house sooner, FHA is your best bet, but be prepared for higher mortgage insurance premiums.
