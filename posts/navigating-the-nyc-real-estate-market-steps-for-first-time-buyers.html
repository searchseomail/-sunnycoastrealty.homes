Navigating the NYC Real Estate Market: Steps for First-Time Buyers

RogueRebel
Topic Starter
Posted: 08.01.2025 09:23
I am currently looking into purchasing my first home in New York City and feel a bit overwhelmed by the complexity of the process. Could anyone provide a clear roadmap on how to start, specifically regarding co-ops versus condos and the typical down payment requirements? I would also appreciate recommendations for reliable real estate agents or mortgage brokers who specialize in the NYC market. Any advice on hidden costs or common pitfalls to avoid would be incredibly helpful.

Welcome to the NYC market! The biggest difference is that co-ops are corporations where you buy shares, while condos are real property. Co-ops often require 20% down and have strict board interviews, whereas condos are more flexible. If you are also looking for international real estate investment options, keep in mind how different those markets are compared to NYC.

Don't forget the 'mansion tax' if your purchase price is over $1M. It's a 1% surcharge that catches many first-time buyers off guard. Always budget for closing costs, which can range from 2% to 6% of the purchase price.

I highly recommend getting a pre-approval letter before you even look at a listing. In NYC, sellers won't take you seriously without one, especially in competitive neighborhoods.
