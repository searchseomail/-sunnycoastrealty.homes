What types of real estate are eligible for the Family Mortgage program?

NeonNomad
Topic Starter
Posted: 05.01.2025 02:46
I am planning to apply for a family mortgage soon and I am trying to understand the current eligibility criteria for property types. Is it possible to use this program for purchasing a secondary market apartment, or am I strictly limited to new developments from developers? I would also like to know if there are any specific requirements for townhouses or individual houses if I decide to go that route. Any advice or recent experiences from forum members would be greatly appreciated.
