Is it safe to buy a property that is currently under mortgage?

RogueRebel
Topic Starter
Posted: 10.01.2025 03:55
I am looking at an apartment that I really like, but the seller informed me that it is still under a mortgage with their bank. I am worried about the legal risks and the process of clearing the title before the sale is finalized. What are the essential steps I should take to ensure my down payment is protected and the lien is properly removed? Has anyone here successfully navigated this type of transaction before?
