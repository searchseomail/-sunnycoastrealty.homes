Looking for the best coastal locations to invest in property – advice needed

CosmicCoder
Topic Starter
Posted: 03.01.2025 01:28
I am currently looking to purchase a vacation home near the sea and am struggling to decide on the best region. My budget is around 200k, and I am prioritizing a warm climate, good infrastructure, and potential for rental income during the off-season. Does anyone have experience buying property in Spain, Greece, or perhaps Southeast Asia? I would appreciate any advice on legal pitfalls or specific coastal towns that are worth considering.
