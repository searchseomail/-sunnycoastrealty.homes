Real estate prices in Montenegro: Is it still a good investment?

HyperHacker
Topic Starter
Posted: 21.01.2025 01:31
I am currently looking into purchasing a holiday home in Montenegro and would like to understand the current market trends. Could someone provide a realistic estimate of what I should expect to pay for a one-bedroom apartment near the coast? I am also curious if the prices vary significantly between areas like Budva, Kotor, and Tivat. Any advice or recent experiences from buyers would be greatly appreciated.

Prices have definitely spiked in the last two years. For a decent one-bedroom in Budva, you are looking at anywhere from €130,000 to €200,000 depending on how close you are to the Old Town.

Tivat is currently the most expensive due to the Porto Montenegro effect. It’s luxury living, so expect to pay a premium. If you are just starting your search, check out these best websites for purchasing property to get a better sense of current listings.

Kotor is beautiful but the inventory is very limited because of the UNESCO protection status. You pay for the history and the views, not necessarily modern amenities.
PixelPioneer

I bought in Bar last year. It is much more affordable than Tivat or Budva and has a more local, year-round vibe. Definitely consider it if you don't need to be in the tourist hotspots.

Montenegro is still a good investment if you plan for long-term rental income, but don't expect the massive capital gains we saw five years ago. The market is maturing.
