Topic Starter

I am currently looking into international real estate investment opportunities but I'm worried about legal restrictions for non-residents. Many countries in the Middle East and parts of Asia have strict laws. China, for instance, doesn't allow private land ownership for anyone, including citizens; you only get land-use rights.

Switzerland is notoriously difficult for non-residents to buy property in due to the 'Lex Koller' regulations. You usually need a special permit unless you are buying in specific tourist zones.

Mexico has a 'Restricted Zone' within 50km of the coast or 100km of the border where foreigners cannot hold direct title to land. You have to use a 'fideicomiso' (bank trust) to purchase property there.
