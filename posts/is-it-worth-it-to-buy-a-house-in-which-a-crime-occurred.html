Is it worth it to buy a house in which a crime occurred?

SleekScout
Topic Starter
Posted: 03.02.2025 11:28
I recently found a beautiful property at a great price, but I discovered that a serious crime took place inside the house a few years ago. I am torn because the location and condition are perfect, but the history of the place makes me feel uneasy. Does anyone have experience with buying stigmatized properties, and does the stigma ever really go away for future resale? I would appreciate any advice on how to handle the psychological aspect and whether it actually impacts long-term value.

You should check your local disclosure laws. Some states don't even require sellers to mention it if it happened more than three years ago. It’s definitely not as uncommon as you think, unlike looking for budget-friendly real estate in this market.
