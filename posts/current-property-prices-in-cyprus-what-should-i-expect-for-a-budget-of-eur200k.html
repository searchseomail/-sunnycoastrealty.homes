Current property prices in Cyprus: What should I expect for a budget of €200k?

MysticMaverick
Topic Starter
Posted: 27.04.2025 00:23
I am currently exploring the possibility of relocating to Cyprus and would like to know the current state of the real estate market. Specifically, what kind of apartment or small house can I realistically expect to purchase with a budget of around €200,000? Are there significant price differences between coastal cities like Limassol versus more affordable areas like Paphos or Larnaca? I would appreciate any advice on hidden costs such as legal fees and taxes that I should factor into my total budget.

Don't forget to factor in about 5-7% on top of the purchase price for legal fees, transfer fees, and stamp duty. If you are looking for residency options, you might want to compare requirements elsewhere, like buying property in Bulgaria for a D Visa, though Cyprus is a great choice for lifestyle.

I live in Paphos and €200k is a solid budget here. You can get a modern 2-bedroom apartment with a communal pool. Limassol is currently booming and very overpriced for the average buyer, so I'd stick to Paphos or Larnaca if you want more value for money.

Make sure you hire an independent lawyer who doesn't work for the developer. Hidden costs like VAT (which is 19% unless you qualify for a reduced 5% rate on your first home) can catch you off guard. Always research the legal landscape before committing.

In Larnaca, your €200k will go much further than in Limassol or Nicosia. You can find newer builds in Oroklini or Livadia within that price range. It's a great time to evaluate your options, especially if you are comparing global markets to see if US housing prices will drop or if Cyprus remains the better stable bet.

Limassol is out of reach for a quality house at €200k. You would be looking at older apartments in need of renovation. Paphos has much more inventory for that price, including small townhouses in quiet residential areas.
