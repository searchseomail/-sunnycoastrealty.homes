Can non-EU citizens legally purchase property in Spain?

SolarSurfer
Topic Starter
Posted: 13.01.2025 06:17
I am currently exploring investment opportunities and am very interested in buying a holiday home in the Costa del Sol region. I am a non-EU citizen and I am confused about the specific legal requirements and restrictions that might apply to me. Could anyone clarify if there are any special permits needed or if the process differs significantly for foreigners? I would appreciate any advice or personal experiences regarding the buying process.

Yes, you absolutely can buy property in Spain as a non-EU citizen. There are no major restrictions on foreign ownership, but you will need to obtain a NIE (Foreigner's Identity Number) first. It’s a straightforward process, though I'd recommend hiring a local gestor to handle the paperwork for you.

You don't need a special permit to buy, but keep in mind that being a non-EU citizen doesn't automatically grant you residency. You'll need to look into the Golden Visa program if you plan to stay for longer periods than the standard 90-day tourist allowance.

It is quite simple, actually. You just need your passport and a NIE. Some people get confused by international laws, but Spain is very welcoming to foreign investors. It’s definitely less complex than buying property in Abkhazia: legal nuances for Russian citizens, where the regulations are much more restrictive.

Don't forget about the taxes! As a non-resident, you will have to pay non-resident income tax (IRNR) on the property, even if you aren't renting it out. It's a small annual fee, but definitely factor it into your budget.
