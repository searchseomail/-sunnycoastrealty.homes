StaticSpecter
Topic Starter
Posted: 10.01.2025 11:33
I am currently looking to diversify my portfolio and am considering buying commercial real estate in Los Angeles. I have been scouting areas like Culver City and Downtown LA, but I'm unsure about the current market volatility and zoning regulations for retail spaces. Could anyone provide advice on which neighborhoods currently offer the best ROI for small-scale commercial investments? Any tips on finding reliable local brokers or navigating the city's permitting process would be greatly appreciated.
