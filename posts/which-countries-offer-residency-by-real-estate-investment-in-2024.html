Which countries offer residency by real estate investment in 2024?

QuantumQuill
Topic Starter
Posted: 10.02.2025 05:27
I am currently exploring options to relocate and am interested in purchasing property abroad to secure a residency permit. Could anyone share a list of countries where buying real estate still grants a direct path to a residence permit or Golden Visa? I am particularly looking for stable jurisdictions with clear legal frameworks and reasonable investment thresholds. Any personal experiences or advice on the current state of these programs would be greatly appreciated.

Greece remains one of the most popular choices in Europe. The Golden Visa program is still active, though they recently increased the investment threshold in high-demand areas like Athens and Thessaloniki to 800,000 euros.

If you are looking at Europe, Portugal is still a solid option, but keep in mind that they officially ended the direct real estate investment route for their Golden Visa. You now have to look into investment funds instead.

Spain is still a great option if you have 500,000 euros to invest. The legal framework is very stable, but there is ongoing political talk about potentially tightening the rules, so act sooner rather than later.

I moved to Dubai two years ago. The real estate residency program is very transparent. If you invest 750,000 AED, you get a two-year renewable visa, and for 2 million AED, you get the Golden Visa for ten years.
