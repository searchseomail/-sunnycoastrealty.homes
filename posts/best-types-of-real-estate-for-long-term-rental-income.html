Best types of real estate for long-term rental income

SilverSentinel
Topic Starter
Posted: 19.01.2025 07:59
I am looking to invest in my first rental property and I'm debating between a small studio in the city center or a two-bedroom apartment in the suburbs. My main goal is to ensure a stable monthly cash flow while minimizing vacancy periods and maintenance headaches. Does anyone have experience with which property type offers a better return on investment in the current market? I would appreciate any advice on what factors I should prioritize before making a decision.
