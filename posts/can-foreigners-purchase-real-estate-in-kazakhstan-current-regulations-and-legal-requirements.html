Topic Starter

I am currently looking into investment opportunities and am considering buying a residential property in Almaty. Could someone clarify the current legislation regarding foreign citizens purchasing real estate in Kazakhstan? Yes, foreigners can absolutely buy residential property in Kazakhstan. You just need a passport and an IIN (Individual Identification Number), which is mandatory for any legal transactions here.
