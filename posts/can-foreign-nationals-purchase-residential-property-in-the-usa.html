Can foreign nationals purchase residential property in the USA?

VelvetVortex
Topic Starter
Posted: 09.01.2025 05:58
I am a non-resident looking to invest in US real estate and would like to know if there are any specific legal restrictions for foreigners. Do I need a visa or a green card to legally buy a home, or can I complete the transaction as a pure investment from abroad? I am also curious if there are any additional tax implications or financing difficulties that I should be aware of before starting the process.

Replies (13)

VoidVoyager
Replied: 07.02.2025 05:06
Yes, you can absolutely buy property in the US as a non-resident. You don't need a visa or green card to own real estate here, though it doesn't automatically grant you any immigration status. It's a straightforward process if you have the funds ready.
RustRider
Replied: 23.02.2025 09:15
You can complete the transaction entirely from abroad, but you'll need an ITIN (Individual Taxpayer Identification Number) for tax reporting purposes. The process is much simpler than buying in some other countries where restrictions are tighter, like buying property in Abkhazia, which is far more complicated.
KrypticKnight
Replied: 24.02.2025 08:39
Financing is the biggest hurdle. Most major US banks are hesitant to lend to non-residents without a credit history. Expect to put down at least 30-40% if you manage to find a lender who works with international clients.

Don't forget about FIRPTA. When you eventually sell the property, the IRS will withhold a percentage of the gross sale price to ensure you pay your capital gains taxes. Always consult a tax professional who understands international real estate.
