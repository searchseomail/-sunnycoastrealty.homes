Best types of commercial real estate for long-term investment?

ZenithZephyr
Topic Starter
Posted: 10.02.2025 09:07
I am currently looking to diversify my portfolio and am considering entering the commercial real estate market. I have a moderate budget and am trying to decide between retail spaces, office units, or small industrial warehouses. Which of these segments currently offers the best balance of stability and rental yield in the current economic climate? Any insights from experienced investors on the potential risks and management overhead for each would be greatly appreciated.
