At what age can you legally purchase real estate?

RogueRebel
Topic Starter
Posted: 30.03.2025 16:26
I am currently 17 years old and have been saving up money from my freelance work to invest in a small apartment. I am wondering if there are any legal restrictions regarding the age at which one can officially buy property in their own name. Can a minor sign a purchase agreement, or do I need to wait until I turn 18 to finalize the deal? Any advice or insights on the legal process for young investors would be greatly appreciated.
