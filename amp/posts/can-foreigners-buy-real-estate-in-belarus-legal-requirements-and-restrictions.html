Yes, foreigners can generally purchase real estate in Belarus, but you cannot own the land itself if it's agricultural or specific types of state-owned plots. You usually get a long-term lease for the land under your property instead. It's a bit complex, so definitely check out this guide on buying property abroad before committing funds.

The registration process is handled through the National Cadastral Agency. You will need to obtain a taxpayer identification number (UNP) in Belarus before you can finalize the purchase. It is not too difficult, but it takes time.
