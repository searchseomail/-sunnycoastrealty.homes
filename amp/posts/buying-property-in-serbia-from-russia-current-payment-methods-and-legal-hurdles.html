Transferring funds directly from a Russian bank to a Serbian account is nearly impossible due to SWIFT restrictions. Most people I know are using intermediary accounts in countries like Kazakhstan or Turkey. You can definitely complete the purchase remotely using a Power of Attorney (Punomoć), but it must be notarized and apostilled. Make sure you find a reputable lawyer in Belgrade to draft it specifically for the property purchase to avoid any legal loopholes.
