The reason you can't own land is due to the Thai Land Code, which prohibits foreigners from owning freehold land in their own name. It's a protectionist policy to prevent land speculation. Condos are the exception! Under the Condominium Act, you can own a unit freehold in your name as long as the total foreign ownership in the building does not exceed 49%. Always check the building's foreign quota first.

Leasehold is essentially a long-term rental, usually 30 years with options to renew. You don't own the land, but you do own the right to use it. It's safer than the company structure, which is legally gray and heavily scrutinized by authorities now.
