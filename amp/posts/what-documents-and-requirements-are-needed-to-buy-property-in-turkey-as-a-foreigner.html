Hi! Alanya and Antalya are wonderful choices for both investment and vacations. To buy property as a foreigner, you will need your valid foreign passport, its notarized translation into Turkish, biometric photos, a Turkish tax number (Vergi Numarası), and a local bank account. The bank account is strictly mandatory now because all foreign currency must be exchanged into Turkish Lira via the Central Bank to get the Dabas document.

The Title Deed transfer (Tapu) usually takes anywhere from 3 to 5 working days once all documents are submitted to the Land Registry Directorate (Tapu Kadastro Müdürlüğü). However, you also need to factor in time for the DÖSİM report, military clearance if required for certain areas, and the sworn appraiser's valuation report.

Make sure your appraiser report (Ekspertiz Raporu) is ready before you transfer any large deposits! The government made property valuation mandatory for all foreigners, and the declared purchase price cannot be lower than the appraised value. This directly affects your property tax and any future residency applications.
