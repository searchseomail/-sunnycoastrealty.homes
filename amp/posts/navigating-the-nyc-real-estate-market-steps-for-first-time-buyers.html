Welcome to the NYC market! The biggest difference is that co-ops are corporations where you buy shares, while condos are real property. Co-ops often require 20% down and have strict board interviews, whereas condos are more flexible. If you are also looking for international real estate investment options, keep in mind how different those markets are compared to NYC.
